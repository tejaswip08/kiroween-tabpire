# Tabpire

A Chrome/Firefox new-tab extension for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon (Costume Contest).

Every open tab is a vampire draining memory. States: Awake, Starving (idle 1+ day), Asleep (discarded), Protected (garlic site).

Actions: Stake (close), Coffin (discard), Crypt (save + close), Garlic (protect site), Resurrect, Dawn (close all starving, safely).

Spooky elements must always mean something; never decoration only.

Local-first: tab data never leaves the browser.
